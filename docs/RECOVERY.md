# Inert-block recovery

The development snapshot implements M3 recovery for canonical `minecraft:stone`
and `minecraft:air`, codec 1/data version 5023, on pinned Paper 26.3 build 248.
The published `v0.1.0-alpha.1` remains a logging/investigation release and has no
recovery commands. M3 completion evidence is tracked in [M3_PLAN.md](M3_PLAN.md).
M5/M6 and any new release remain separate gates.

## Scope and commands

Grant `chronicle.rollback` and/or `chronicle.restore` explicitly. Both default to
false, including for operators. Local console is trusted. Admission, confirmation,
execution and asynchronous delivery recheck the relevant permission; logout stops
the requester's execution. `chronicle.status` allows operation summaries.

```text
/chronicle rollback preview time:1h world:world origin:0,0 radius:20 action:block-place,block-break player:Alex
/chronicle rollback confirm <preview-id>
/chronicle restore preview <rollback-id>
/chronicle restore confirm <preview-id>
/chronicle status operation:<operation-id>
/chronicle rollback pause
/chronicle rollback review <operation-id>
/chronicle restore review <operation-id>
/chronicle rollback continue <paused-rollback-id>
/chronicle restore continue <paused-restore-id>
/chronicle rollback compensate <uncertain-rollback-id>
```

`continue` and `compensate` create new previews. Confirm with the corresponding
`rollback confirm` or `restore confirm`; neither command resumes an old intent. Restore pause uses
`restore pause`. Review is read-only and prints each step's source, status, actual
snapshots when known, position, current-state comparison and reason in bounded
slices. SQL/worker failures are errors, not empty results.

Rollback requires explicit time, world, radius and action filters. Player origin
can be copied at submission; console needs `origin:x,z`. Filter semantics and UUID
resolution follow [investigation](INVESTIGATION.md). Omitted player means **all
actors**, explicitly highlighted in the resolved scope. Radius is at
most 100; selection is at most **1,000 changes**. Oversized selections fail and
must be narrowed. These are conservative development bounds, below the original
10,000-row proposal; M5 must justify any increase.

One preview/execution context is retained globally. A new permitted preview can
replace a waiting preview. A prepared/paused operation must finish or be explicitly
reviewed before another execution starts. The preview freezes resolved filters,
selected IDs, source relationships, capture barrier, committed boundary, expected
and desired snapshots, owner and SHA-256 digest. Expiry is 60 seconds from request,
also enforced by a monotonic in-memory deadline. Restart expires waiting previews.
Confirmation never reruns filters. Repetition reports the same operation and cannot
apply it again; a busy metadata query can require retrying status.

## Eligibility and application

Only a complete single-member, directly attributed stone/air transition qualifies.
M1/M2 rows and their historical `recovery_eligible=0` evidence remain unchanged;
the versioned planner independently qualifies recovery. It examines the full later
position chain, including unselected actors/actions and operation audit rows.
Original rows behind later operation history are conservatively conflicted;
restoration uses the operation relationship instead of broad filters.

Each target and its complete 3 x 3 x 3 neighbourhood must be loaded and contain only
canonical stone/air. Unknown worlds, unloaded chunks, unsupported metadata,
non-inert neighbours and occupied placement targets are refused. No chunk is
synchronously loaded or generated by recovery. The codec uses public
[`setBlockData`](https://jd.papermc.io/paper/26.3/org/bukkit/block/Block.html) with
physics enabled. It does not cancel physics or use natural breaking. Immediate and
next-tick snapshots must match the expected target and unchanged neighbours.

Application reverses capture order. Fresh history, effective eligibility, target
and neighbour revisions, full state, permission and health are checked after
durable intent acknowledgement. Known edits returning to identical state invalidate
the preview. Cancelled/unsupported observed attempts also invalidate watched
dependencies conservatively. Only synchronous inert-shell physics with the exact
active mutation source is correlated; unrelated events still pass through capture.
A reserved capture slot keeps later player history behind the operation audit.

Coverage is checked in session/capture order, not wall-clock order. Known gaps
crossing a source prevent application. `STARTUP` is diagnostic; actual observations
before readiness record failure. A prior unclean interval blocks older affected
sources, while later complete history can qualify. Public events cannot detect
every direct plugin/NMS mutation or an unobserved change away and back. The controlled
fixture is not universal third-party compatibility.

Recovery creates no drops or XP. It does not reclaim mined items, refund placement
costs or conserve the economy. Inventories, stored loot tables, block entities,
fluids, supports, multi-block structures and arbitrary physics remain excluded.

## Journal and bounds

Schema 3 appends `operations`, `recovery_steps`, `recovery_effects` and audit links
on `changes`; migrations 1/2 and existing history payloads are preserved. Intent
and effective claims commit before a world write. Actual verified before/after
snapshots, applied outcome, audit change and restore eligibility commit atomically
through the existing writer. Retries compare canonical content and cannot create
a second audit/effect. Unknown actual state is nullable evidence, never invented air.

One retained recovery control request, including its undelivered result, runs on
the writer after preceding capture drains. Recovery SQL has a two-second/roughly
ten-million-VM-instruction budget. This conservative serialization can delay
capture commits under expensive planning; queue bounds/backpressure remain active.
M5 mixed-load performance qualification is still required. No server callback waits
for JDBC or filesystem work. Worker timeouts cannot preempt arbitrary native/OS stalls.

World slices stop at 50 steps or two milliseconds of measured work. The executor
currently waits for each outcome acknowledgement before preparing a dependent step;
throughput is deliberately limited. A single API call cannot be preempted and can
overrun two milliseconds. Status reports maximum call/slice duration and overrun
count. The real-server codec fixture observed a roughly 5.6 ms cold call; the budget
is a scheduling cutoff, not a hard maximum tick duration.

Applied, skipped, failed, unknown and unattempted steps remain separately visible.
Permission loss, operator pause, unavailable storage or uncertainty stop further
mutation. Shutdown pauses prepared/applying operations in the same transaction as
the clean logging-session seal. A failed/partial operation never deletes history.

## Restore and restart review

Restore references a rollback ID and only its verified `APPLIED`, unrestored steps.
It reverses actual recorded snapshots in reverse application order. Skipped, failed,
unattempted and unknown steps are ineligible. Transactional claims prevent double
rollback or restoration. Fresh live checks run even for retained completed effects
from a clean previous session.

SQLite commit and Minecraft world save are separate durability boundaries. Startup
turns unresolved intents into `UNKNOWN` and unfinished operations into
`NEEDS_RECONCILIATION`; nothing resumes automatically. Completed effects retain their
original verification session and must be checked live before restoration.

Review distinguishes expected state (not applied or save reverted), desired state
(possibly applied), conflicting state and unavailable targets. Equality cannot prove
causation. Keep independent stopped-server backups and preserve the journal/WAL.
An unclean coverage interval or later history remains a blocker even after review;
review is not a gap waiver.

Continuation selects only unattempted rollback/restore steps into a fresh plan,
preserves the original restore-source links and marks transferred steps explicitly.
Compensation selects uncertain rollback steps
into a separate inverse plan, with fresh history/state/dependency checks and explicit
confirmation. It requires the desired state to be present and usable evidence;
expected, conflicting, unavailable or gap-crossing targets are excluded. Compensation
does **not** promote the uncertain source to verified history or make it restorable.
Unresolved uncertainty stays pinned for operator review. There is no automatic replay,
automatic compensation, force override, retention or backup command.

The tests exercise process death and controlled world-save divergence. They do not
simulate hardware power loss, establish storage-device sync honesty, or qualify
production capacity. See [testing](TESTING.md) and [storage](STORAGE.md).
